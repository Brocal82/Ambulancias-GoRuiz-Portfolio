# Sick leave (admin)

**Route:** `/admin/sick-leaves`  
**Required module:** `sick-leaves`

---

## What you can do

- View **all sick leave** for the company (active and closed).
- Open details: dates, worker, attached documents.
- Manage status according to your company's workflow (pending, active, closed).
- View or download **medical certificates** (PDF/image) securely from the panel.

---

## Typical workflow

1. The worker reports sick leave from the app → [Worker sick leave](../worker/BAJAS.md).
2. They may attach medical documentation.
3. The admin reviews at `/admin/sick-leaves`.
4. Adjust shift scheduling if the leave affects already published days.

---

## Privacy

Sick leave documents are **confidential**. Only the worker and authorized admins from the same company can open them through the system (not public links).

---

## Indicators in lists

Some views show **document counts** or visual sick leave status. Use them to prioritize pending reviews.
