# Mechanics and breakdowns (admin)

**Route:** `/admin/mechanics`  
**Required module:** `mechanics`

---

## What you can do

- View **reported breakdowns** from workers or mechanics.
- Create and update **work orders**.
- Assign status (open, in progress, closed, etc. depending on screens).
- Manage links to affected **ambulances**.

---

## Where breakdowns come from

Workers can report from the app → [Report breakdown](../worker/REPORTAR-AVERIA.md).

**Mechanic** and **head mechanic** roles have their own screens at `/mechanics` (not covered in detail in this company admin manual; central company review is usually at `/admin/mechanics`).

---

## Best practices

- Close orders when the repair is verified.
- Keep the [Ambulance catalog](./CATALOGOS.md) up to date to associate each case correctly.
