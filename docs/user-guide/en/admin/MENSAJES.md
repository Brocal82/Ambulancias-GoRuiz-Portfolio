# Messages (admin)

**Routes:** `/admin/messages`, `/admin/messages/sent`  
**Module:** messages (usually always available)

---

## Send messages

1. Open **Messages**.
2. Write subject and body.
3. Choose **recipients** (workers or groups depending on the screen).
4. Optional: attach files (images or PDF within limits).
5. Send.

Workers receive the message in the app (**Messages** tab) and may get a **push notification** if the app is installed.

Worker guide: [Messages](../worker/MENSAJES.md).

---

## Inbox and sent

- **Received:** messages others have sent you (if applicable to admins).
- **Sent** (`/admin/messages/sent`): history of outgoing communications.

---

## Attachments

Attachments are not public links. They are opened securely inside the application.

---

## Best practices

- Use clear subjects.
- For urgent notices (shift changes), combine the message with verbal confirmation when critical.
- Respect privacy: do not send sensitive medical data by message if they belong in the sick leave module.
