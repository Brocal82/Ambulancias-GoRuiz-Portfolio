# Schedule and shifts (worker)

**Tab:** Schedule (app) or web route `/dienst` if using the browser

---

## What you see

- Your **assigned shifts** by day and week.
- Times, shift number (Dienst), and data your company publishes (ambulance, team, etc.).

The schedule is prepared by the administrator → [Admin shifts](../admin/TURNOS.md).

---

## How to use it

1. Open **Schedule**.
2. Navigate by weeks or days (depending on the screen).
3. Before your shift, check time and assignment.
4. On shift day, go to **Workday** to record activity → [Workday](./JORNADA.md).

---

## Excel planning

If your company imports shifts from Excel, the schedule updates after admin publication. You do not need to do anything special in the app.

---

## If a shift is missing

Contact your administrator. The week may not be published yet or you may not be assigned that day.
