# Sick leave (worker)

**Section:** Sick leave  
**Module:** `sick-leaves`

---

## Report sick leave

1. Open **Sick leave**.
2. Enter **start date** and **end date** (or estimated end).
3. Optional: **attach** medical certificate (photo or PDF).
4. Submit.

---

## Follow-up

- Check the **status** of your sick leave in the same section.
- The administrator reviews documents and dates → [Admin sick leave](../admin/BAJAS.md).

---

## Privacy

Your medical documents are visible only to you and authorized administrators from your company, within the app.

---

## During sick leave

Do not record a **workday** on sick leave days unless your company tells you otherwise. Coordinate with your admin if you had assigned shifts.
