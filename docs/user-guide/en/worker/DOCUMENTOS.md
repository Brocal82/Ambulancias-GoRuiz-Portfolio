# Documents (worker)

**Access:** from Home or Documents section  
**Module:** `documents`

---

## What they are

Documents your company **delivers** to you: circulars, protocols, forms, etc.

---

## How to use them

1. Open **Documents**.
2. You will see only what was sent to you.
3. Open each document to read it (PDF or other allowed format).
4. If **read confirmation** is required, follow the button or action shown on screen.

Admin guide: [Admin documents](../admin/DOCUMENTOS.md).

---
