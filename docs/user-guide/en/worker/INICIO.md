# Getting started in the app (worker)

## Install the app

1. Download **Ambulancias GoRuiz** from the store your company indicates (iOS / Android).
2. Open the app.

---

## First sign-in

1. If you received an **invitation link**, open it first in the browser, complete registration, then sign in to the app with the same email and password.
2. On the **login** screen, enter email and password.
3. After a successful login, you will see **Home** with access to the sections.

**Note:** Administrator accounts cannot use the worker app; you will see a block message.

---

## Bottom tabs (typical)

| Tab | Function |
|-----|----------|
| **Home** | Quick access (documents, bonuses, etc.) |
| **Schedule** | Your shifts |
| **Workday** | Record the day (if module is active) |
| **Messages** | Communications (may show unread badge) |
| **Profile** | Your details and sign out |

Other tabs (vacation, sick leave, appointments) may appear depending on active modules or from Home.

---

## Language

If your company uses several languages, the language may follow the device or web configuration. Guides are available in Spanish, German, and English in `docs/user-guide/`.

---

## Notifications

The first time, the app may ask for **notification** permission. Accept if you want alerts for new messages or other company notices.

---

## Sign out

**Profile** → sign out. Sign in again when you change devices or share the phone.

---

## Common issues

| Issue | What to do |
|-------|------------|
| Incorrect password | Check caps lock; ask your admin for a reset |
| No shifts in schedule | Admin must generate the week and assign you |
| Session expired | Sign in again |
| "Administrator account" | Use the web panel, not the worker app |
