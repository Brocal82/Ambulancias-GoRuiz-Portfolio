# Workday and trips (worker)

**Tab:** Workday (module `workday`)

---

## Purpose

Record your day's activity: start, trips to hospitals, ambulance used, and **closing** each segment. Your company will review the summary afterward.

Admin guide: [Workday review](../admin/JORNADA.md).

---

## Daily workflow

1. **Open Workday** on the day you have an assigned shift.
2. **Start** the workday or first trip (depending on your app version).
3. During service, record each **trip**:
   - Destination hospital (if applicable)
   - Ambulance
   - Start and end times
   - Optional notes
4. **Close** each trip when finished.
5. At end of day, complete **workday closure** if the app requests it.

---

## Tips

- Record in real time so you do not forget hours.
- Check that ambulance and hospital are correct.
- If you cannot close, check internet connection and try again.

---

## After closing

The administrator **reviews** the workday. Confirmed hours may be used for **bonuses** → [Bonuses](./PRIMAS.md).

---

## Common issues

| Issue | What to do |
|-------|------------|
| Workday does not appear | Module not active or no shift that day |
| Cannot close | Complete required fields; contact admin |
| Incorrect hours | Notify admin before they close the workday definitively |
