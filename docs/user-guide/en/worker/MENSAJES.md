# Messages (worker)

**Tab:** Messages

---

## Receive messages

- Messages from your company appear in the list.
- The tab **badge** shows unread items (you may also get a push notification).
- Open a message to read it in full.

---

## Attachments

If there are attachments, open them from the app. They download securely (not public links).

---

## Send messages

If your company allows it, you can write messages to indicated recipients. Follow internal communication rules.

Admin guide: [Admin messages](../admin/MENSAJES.md).

---

## Notifications

With the app in the background, a new message may show a notification. Tapping it usually takes you straight to **Messages**.
