# Payroll (worker)

**Access:** from Home or Payroll section (web `/worker/payroll`)  
**Module:** `payroll`

---

## What you see

- List of **your payrolls** by month/year that the administrator uploaded and assigned to your user.
- Only **active** documents (not invalidated versions).

---

## Open a payroll

1. Select the period.
2. Tap to **open** the PDF.
3. The app downloads the file securely and lets you view or share it depending on the system.

---

## If a payroll is missing

Contact administration. It may be pending upload, unassigned, or incorrectly matched.

Admin guide: [Admin payroll](../admin/NOMINAS.md).
