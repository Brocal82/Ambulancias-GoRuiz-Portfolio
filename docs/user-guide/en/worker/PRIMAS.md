# Bonuses (worker)

**Access:** from Home or Bonuses section  
**Module:** `praemien`

---

## What you see

- Summary of **bonuses** for months already **confirmed** or **paid** by your company.
- In automatic mode, they depend on workdays you recorded and the admin reviewed.
- In manual mode, the admin enters the amounts.

Admin guide: [Admin bonuses](../admin/PRIMAS.md).

---

## If a bonus is missing

- The month may still be in draft.
- Workdays may not be reviewed yet.
- The module may not be active.

Ask your administrator with the specific month.
